Designed and built an assistive navigation cane for visually impaired users, powered by a Raspberry Pi Pico (RP2040).

**Sensing:** Ultrasonic sensor arrays cover a 170-degree forward cone and detect drop-offs below the cane tip — addressing both forward obstacles and step/curb hazards that standard canes miss.

**Feedback:** Detected obstacles and drop-offs are mapped to haptic motors on the cane handle and a wearable wrist bracelet, giving the user directional and distance-coded feedback without audio distraction.

**Skills:** RP2040 firmware, ultrasonic ranging, haptic actuators, embedded C, wearable hardware.
