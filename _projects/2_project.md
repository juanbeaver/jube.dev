Built a motorized capture rig for at-home volumetric limb tracking using unconventional image capture and 3D reconstruction.

**Hardware:** ESP32 + stepper driver assembly with Fusion 360/Onshape mounts, Li-ion DC/DC power supply, and bench-tested calibration for stable motion and consistent data capture.

**Software:** Implemented a Structure-from-Motion (SfM) pipeline to reconstruct 3D arm meshes from images captured by the rig. The system targets at-home volumetric tracking for lymphedema monitoring without clinical-grade 3D scanners.

**Skills:** ESP32 firmware, stepper motor control, Fusion 360, SfM/photogrammetry, Python.
