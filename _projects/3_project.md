Built a CNN-based vision model to detect manipulator–skin contact for minimally invasive telesurgery. The model infers applied force via contact detection and local stiffness models, removing the need for embedded force sensors on the surgical tool.

**Model & Data:** Curated and optimized a 200,000+ image dataset. Built a custom Amazon Mechanical Turk labeling GUI to collect ground-truth contact annotations efficiently. Training optimizations reduced training time while improving accuracy.

**Publication:** Co-authored in *Journal of Medical Robotics Research* (2024) — {% cite yang2024vision %}.

**Skills:** PyTorch, CNNs, CUDA, dataset curation, Python, computer vision.
