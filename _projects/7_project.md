Built an adaptive RC car controller to give children with cerebral palsy independent access to ride-on toy vehicles, as part of a Design For America team building accessible play devices.

**Hardware:** A microcontroller drives the OEM RC board's inputs directly, replacing the stock controls with up to four 3.5 mm switch jacks so kids can drive using whatever switches best match their motor abilities. USB-powered and housed in a 3D-printed enclosure for durability and easy handling.

**Process & Deployment:** Worked directly with United Cerebral Palsy to test the controller with kids and iterate on the design. Led a 5–7 person team through the build; deployed 3 controllers over one semester.

**Skills:** Embedded firmware, switch-adapted I/O design, 3D-printed enclosure design, user testing, team leadership.
